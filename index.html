Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before travelling to Kenya.

When is the best time to see the Great Migration in Maasai Mara?

+

The best time to witness the Mara River crossings is between July and October, though wildlife viewing in Kenya is spectacular year-round.

Are park entrance fees included in the tour price?

+

Yes! All our listed package prices include KWS park entry fees, full-board lodge accommodation, and daily game drives.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept Visa, Mastercard, bank wire transfers, and M-Pesa for both local and international bookings.
